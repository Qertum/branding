01The markA monogram built from cryptographic geometry, not decoration.

Two interlocking rings, sealed — with a single bar turned outward to form the Q.

The mark reads as a precision mechanism: two concentric, segmented rings whose dashes share one rhythm and interleave like a lock. One outer segment is rotated ninety degrees into a radial tail — the only break in the system — which resolves the form into a Q.

It nods to the lattices and layered key exchange at the heart of post-quantum cryptography while staying abstract enough to live as a favicon, an app tile, or an embossed seal.
